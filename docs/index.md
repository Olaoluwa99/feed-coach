**Steer your YouTube homepage toward what you want, and see which of YouTube's own buttons actually work for you.**

Feed Coach is a free, open-source Chrome extension. It shows what your YouTube homepage is made of, lets you set goals (more of some topics, less of others), suggests which of YouTube's own buttons to use, and then measures whether your feed changed and whether each button worked. It never clicks anything on YouTube for you.

Everything it records stays on your computer. There's no Feed Coach server, no account and no analytics.
